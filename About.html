Musician and composer Kristian Enkerud Lien from Otta, Norway (1996) is exploring creative music. This can be heard in the following bands: KRISE, Idiotek, ükya, Traum, vårknipa, Dahl/Nilssen-Love/Enkerud Lien, Ellerhusen Holm/Enkerud Lien and as a solo performer.
